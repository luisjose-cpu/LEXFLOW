# PRODUCT_SCOPE.md

## Product

**LEXFLOW - The Legal Operating System** is a SaaS platform for law firms that currently operate across spreadsheets, cloud folders, email, and chat.

## Target Users

- Law firm partners
- Associates and legal operators
- Administrative legal staff
- Clients accessing case status through a portal
- Firm managers reviewing operational intelligence

## Core Modules

1. Expediente 360
2. Portal Cliente
3. WhatsApp Business and multichannel communication
4. Practical legal AI
5. Authorized judicial automation
6. Legal Intelligence Center
7. Legal Command Center
8. PWA and mobile experience
9. SaaS Billing
10. Automation Studio
11. Hardening and QA
12. LEXFLOW OS

## Product Spine

The product must connect:

`CLIENTE -> EXPEDIENTE -> DOCUMENTO -> COMUNICACION -> AUTOMATIZACION -> IA -> INTELIGENCIA -> DECISION`

## In Scope For P0

- Governance documents
- Architecture principles
- Security principles
- QA rules
- Roadmap
- Risks
- P1 readiness criteria
- Folder structure for future work

## Out Of Scope For P0

- New frontend functionality
- New backend functionality
- Database tables
- Docker functionality
- Production deployments
- External integrations
- AI implementation
- Billing implementation
