# P10 Legal Command Center

## Goal

Create an executive dashboard for partners and management covering operation, risk, clients, productivity, cases, legal intelligence, judicial updates, AI, and communications.

## KPIs

- Active cases.
- Critical cases.
- Cases without movement.
- Hearings.
- Pending documents.
- Clients.
- Messages.
- AI jobs and review queue.
- Legal news.
- Productivity and workload.
- CAPTCHA pending.

## Frontend

- `/dashboard`

The command center is a working executive view, not a placeholder.
