# P9 Centro de Inteligencia Juridica

## Goal

Create a legal intelligence center for legal news, regulations, jurisprudence, alerts, official and specialized sources, AI summaries, favorites, tags, and trends.

## Sources

P9 prepares source adapters for:

- LP Derecho
- Juris.pe
- El Peruano
- SPIJ
- Poder Judicial
- MPFN
- SINOE

## Product Rules

- No aggressive scraping.
- Use APIs, RSS, permitted integrations, or mock adapters.
- AI summaries are operational aids and require professional review.
- Intelligence can be linked to cases so Expediente 360 shows related legal context.

## User Experience

The `/legal-intelligence` route provides a dashboard with source status, legal news cards, filters, search, AI summaries, favorite actions, case linking, alerts, and trend visualization.
