# LEVEL3_KNOWLEDGE_VAULT

Knowledge Vault stores reusable legal knowledge: templates, precedents, prompts, strategies and checklists.

APIs:

- `GET /api/v1/knowledge/items`
- `POST /api/v1/knowledge/items`
- `GET /api/v1/knowledge/search`
- `GET /api/v1/knowledge/templates`
- `GET /api/v1/knowledge/precedents`
- `GET /api/v1/knowledge/prompts`

Frontend: `/knowledge`
